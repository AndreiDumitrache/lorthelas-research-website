Entering the late stage of the current market cycle offers us the opportunity to look back at previous cycles to get a better understanding of how cryptocurrency projects will evolve in the near future. Over the past several years we have built a strong foundation consisting of indicators and macroeconomic shifts which helped us provide more insightful predictions.

![](/images/b07d19b0-ec20-44a2-94a1-7022f2065661.jpeg)

In the cryptocurrency industry most projects have very fast development and release cycles which is a phenomenon rarely observed in other industries. These projects evolve in rapid environments and get popular on few (if any) fundamentals. For this reason we are mostly looking to major currencies like Bitcoin for clarity.

According to our indicator analysis, we predict that a cycle top on Bitcoin will occur somewhere between the end of Q3 and middle of Q4 this year. Additionally, these indicator show a price range between $140.000 and $180.000 for one Bitcoin, with more optimistic outlooks reaching over $200.000 if the uptrend gains momentum and keeps it throughout the end of the year. We must note that we do not weigh in heavily on price predictions and we will explore the reason why in the report.

The world in which the industry of cryptocurrencies exists is very rapid and sometimes violent, which makes it difficult to predict the future prices these assets. However, using the technical indicators presented in this report we predict that Bitcoin will see a steady increase in price on higher time frames.
